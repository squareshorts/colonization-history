# Data licence

The contents of `results/` and `empirical/observables/` are released under the
Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0),
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/.

`empirical/observables/` contains measurements (occupancy counts, cell tracks and
per-cell fluorescence readings) derived from the image series deposited by
Lee *et al.* at figshare, doi:10.6084/m9.figshare.5969335, which accompany
Lee *et al.* (2018) Proc. Natl Acad. Sci. USA 115:4471-4476,
doi:10.1073/pnas.1720071115. No raw images are redistributed. Users of the derived
observables should cite the original deposit and article and comply with the
deposit's own licence terms.

All code is released under the MIT licence (`LICENSE`).
