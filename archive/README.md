# Archived experimental and methodological extensions

The `previous_extensions` subtree preserves the complete analysis extensions that preceded the focused carrier-discrimination article. It includes the earlier field-level residence analyses, minimal-agent capability ablations, six-arm counterfactual decomposition, and the exact equivalence and calibration benchmarks. These analyses were retained for transparency and historical reproducibility. Their assumptions and endpoints sometimes differ from the measurements in the current article; consult the local reports before reusing an older numerical result.

Current article source and numerical checks are located under [`../../paper/`](../../paper/). The old source and results remain unchanged under this archive. Git history additionally preserves their original paths and commits.
